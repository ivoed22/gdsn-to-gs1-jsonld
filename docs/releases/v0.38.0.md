# v0.38.0 — Dual review-safe and fully embedded JSON-LD

Every conversion now offers two versions of its JSON-LD output:

1. **Review-safe** preserves each 60%+ candidate as a removable
   `schema:PropertyValue`. The proposed GS1 property is recorded as text and is
   not asserted.
2. **Fully embedded review** also places those candidate values directly under
   their proposed GS1 or Schema.org properties. This is an experimental human
   review artifact; domain, range, nesting and meaning may be wrong.

Both variants are shown on screen and have separate download buttons. The
suggestion panel records the complete reviewer input coverage and makes clear
that all 288 published candidates at 60% or above have consensus metadata,
while the full 3,040-row review universe includes no-equivalent, low-score,
alternative-target and conflicting decisions that are not active candidates.
